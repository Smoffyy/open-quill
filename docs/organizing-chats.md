# Organizing Your Chats

## Folders

The sidebar supports folders: create, rename, delete, and drag chats into them, or use a chat's **…** menu → **Move to folder**. Starred chats always float to the top of the list regardless of folder.

## Starring and archiving

**Star** pins a chat to the top of the sidebar. **Archive** moves it out of the main list without deleting it. Archived chats live in their own tab in the chats overview and can be unarchived any time.

## Chats overview

A full grid view of every chat, with **Active**/**Archived** tabs and infinite scroll. Turn on multi-select to bulk **Archive**, **Unarchive**, or **Delete** across many chats at once.

## Search

`Ctrl+Shift+F` opens a fuzzy search across every chat's title and content, with keyboard navigation between results and highlighted match snippets, so you can find a specific thing you remember saying without recalling which chat it was in.

## Pins

Within a chat, individual messages and attached files can be pinned via the chat menu. Pinned messages give you a quick jump list, and pinned files stay easily reachable without scrolling back through the whole thread.

## Projects

A project groups related chats around shared context. Create one with a name and description, and it gets its own composer for starting new chats inside it, a list of its chats, editable **Instructions** that apply to every chat in the project, and file uploads (PDFs, text, code) that those chats can read and search. Useful for anything with an ongoing body of work, like a codebase, a research topic, or a long-running task, where you don't want to restate context in every new chat.

## Spaces

Spaces are shared, multi-user chat rooms with the assistant, for working with other people rather than alone. An owner creates a space and invites members by name or email search; roles are **Owner**, **Editor**, and **Viewer** (view-only). Inside a space you can @-mention people or the assistant, see live typing indicators, and (as owner) rename the space, change its model or system prompt, or remove members. Invited members see a pending invite they can accept or decline.

## Export and import

**Settings → General → Export everything** downloads a JSON snapshot of your account: chats, folders, styles, personas, saved prompts, memory, and preferences. **Import** merges a snapshot back in. Your profile data is merged and any chats in the file are added; existing chats are never overwritten.
